Doing IT yourself, or about to hire?

One hire is one person. Managed IT is coverage.

If IT currently lands on you, or you are weighing a first IT hire, this is the honest comparison most owners work through.

Hiring one IT person

A single salary carries a lot of single points of failure.

  • One person's hours and one person's knowledge. If they do not know it, it does not get done.
  • Coverage stops when they are sick, on vacation, in training, or if they leave.
  • You carry salary plus payroll taxes and benefits, before any tools or software.
  • Security, backup, and monitoring tools are extra purchases you still have to choose and manage.

Byte Size Solutions

Fully managed, or co-managed alongside your person.

  • Help desk, security, backups, and around-the-clock monitoring included.
  • One flat rate per user per month, simple to budget and typically less than the full cost of one hire.
  • Already have someone on IT? Co-managed support takes the routine load off them so they can focus on the business.
  • Clear monthly reporting, so you can see what was done and what needs attention next.

The right choice depends on your size. For most small businesses, managed IT covers more ground for less than a single hire costs once taxes, benefits, tools, and cover are counted.
